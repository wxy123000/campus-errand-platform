Workflow

Order Status Timeline

Placed

The customer submits the order and receives a confirmation immediately.

Accepted

The order has been taken and is ready to be handled by a runner.

Picked Up

The requested item has been collected and is on the way to the destination.

Delivered

The order is completed after PIN verification or approved photo proof.

Verification and appeal rules

PIN delivery: The customer keeps the one-time PIN and gives it to the runner only at the in-person handover.

Photo proof: For leave-and-photo delivery, the runner uploads a clear delivery photo. The customer can then confirm receipt.

Appeals: Only completed orders can be appealed. The customer or assigned runner may submit one appeal with a reason; once resolved, the same order cannot be appealed again. The current prototype does not impose a separate appeal deadline.

Account restrictions: Customer cancellations and runner give-ups are recorded for administrator review. Administrators may manually apply or remove a three-day account restriction when reviewing misuse.
